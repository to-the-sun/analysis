Calibration Diagrams

One row per tracked element, drawn against the shared time axis by diagram_template.py. Each diagram and its audio are read from this folder.
Drag horizontally on a diagram to zoom into a time range. Right-click to zoom out one level. Click to jump the playhead there and play. Reset Zoom restores the whole file.
Audio seeking needs byte-range support: python3 ../pattern_report_server.py --root . --port 8000, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/report.html.
